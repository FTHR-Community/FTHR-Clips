FAQ

All the popular questions you may have.

System Status Closed Development
01

What is FTHRClips?

FTHRClips is privacy-first clipping software made by us FTHR-Software for saving gameplay moments locally with high performance and no social platform attached.

02

Is FTHRClips available yet?

Not publicly. The project is in closed development, with progress updates shared through the main site and Discord. Alpha release scheduled for Q3 of 2026.

03

Does it collect data?

No. FTHRClips is designed around local capture, local files, no accounts, no telemetry, and no background network calls.

04

Where are clips stored?

Clips are saved to your own machine. The exact folder can be configured in the app once the public build is available.

05

Will it support high framerates and resolutions?

Yes. FTHRClips is built to follow what your hardware can handle instead of enforcing artificial capture caps.

06

Which GPUs are planned?

The capture pipeline targets hardware encoders from NVIDIA, AMD, and Intel, with software fallback for systems without compatible hardware encoding.

07

Will there be a subscription?

The site positions FTHRClips against subscription-locked basics. Core clipping should not require paying for unnecessary platform features.
