Short Version

Gary Clicker does not collect, sell, share, or track your personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ad trackers, or server-side game saves built into the game.

Local Progress

Your game progress, settings, upgrades, scores, and other play state are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. That data stays on your device and is not sent to us by the game.

If you clear your browser storage, use a private browsing mode, switch browsers, or play on another device, your Gary progress may reset or may not be available.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, account details, location data, payment data, gameplay analytics, device identifiers, or cookies through Gary Clicker.

Hosting And Browser Requests

Like any website, the service that hosts Gary Clicker may receive ordinary technical request information, such as IP address, browser type, requested files, and timestamps. Gary Clicker does not use that information to identify you, track you, or save your progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page. If the game ever starts collecting data, this policy should be updated before that happens.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via mail at: contact@fthrclips.com
