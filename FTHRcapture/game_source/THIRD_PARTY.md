FTHR's Windows Minecraft capture provider uses the OBS Studio 32.1.2 Windows x64 game capture module, libobs, the D3D11 renderer and their runtime dependencies. The helper runs in its own process and loads the copies shipped next to it. FTHRGameSource is licensed under GPL-3.0-only. OBS Studio is distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later. See OBS-COPYING.txt.

The OBS [32.1.2 sources](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/tree/fb4d98bf88fae5fc85cb11fc57f7c5e309282194), [build presets](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/blob/fb4d98bf88fae5fc85cb11fc57f7c5e309282194/CMakePresets.json), and [Windows binaries](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/releases/tag/32.1.2) are available upstream.

The provider uses the unmodified, dynamically linked FFmpeg 7.1.3 libraries from the PySide6 6.11.1 Windows x64 distribution. FFmpeg is copyright the FFmpeg developers and licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. The license is included as `LGPL-2.1.txt`. The [FFmpeg 7.1.3 source](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/n7.1.3), [Qt build instructions](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtmultimedia-building-ffmpeg.html), and [Qt attribution and dependency notices](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.11/qtmultimedia-attribution-ffmpeg.html) are available upstream. Recipients may replace these shared libraries and reverse engineer the combination to debug their modifications.

The OBS distribution supplies Pthreads-win32, libcurl and zlib. [Pthreads-win32 source](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/tree/fb4d98bf88fae5fc85cb11fc57f7c5e309282194/deps/w32-pthreads) and its accompanying notices are included upstream. See `Pthreads-Win32-COPYING.txt` and `LGPL-2.1.txt` for its terms. `OBS-deps-NOTICES.txt` includes the [libcurl](https://curl.se/docs/copyright.html) and [zlib 1.3.1](https://github.com/madler/zlib/tree/v1.3.1) license terms. OBS provides its corresponding [dependency build sources](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-deps/tree/2025-08-23).
