Ko-fi promotional brand asset
============================

FTHR_UI/assets/icons/kofi_symbol.png contains Ko-fi's cup symbol and is used
only on the donation button linking to https://ko-fi.com/fthrclips.
Copyright and trademarks belong to Ko-fi / Ko-fi Labs Limited.
This asset is separately licensed and is not covered by FTHR's GPL licence.

Ko-fi's official creator kit offers its logos, buttons and cups for promotion
of a creator's Ko-fi page. Its official help article confirms this permission,
subject to the Ko-fi content guidelines:

https://more.ko-fi.com/brand-assets
https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007169493-Can-I-use-the-Ko-fi-logo-to-promote-my-page
https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007937553-Ko-fi-Content-Guidelines

Verified on 2026-10-13. The existing PNG was compared visually with the cup
symbol published in the official creator kit. Its original acquisition URL
was not recorded; this notice does not claim it was downloaded directly from
Ko-fi or place it under an open-source licence.
