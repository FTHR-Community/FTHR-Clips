FTHR Clips user-provided preview asset
========================================

The file FTHR_UI/assets/preview_desktop.png was supplied by the FTHR Clips
workspace owner for use as the standard visual-overlays preview background.
It is included in the application at the owner's request and is not a
project-generated or third-party asset.

The asset is intended for this workspace and its packaged FTHR Clips builds.
